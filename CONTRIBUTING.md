# Contributing / 参与贡献

Bug reports, translations, documentation and focused patches are welcome. Use Issues for a reproducible defect and Discussions (when enabled) for questions and ideas. Run both test suites listed in docs/en/build.md before proposing code.

By intentionally submitting a contribution, you confirm you have the right to submit it under the project's AGPL-3.0-only terms. Preserve third-party notices and document material modifications. Contributors retain their copyrights; no commercial relicensing rights or copyright transfer are implied. Any future separate commercial agreement would require the necessary permissions.

Do not submit user databases, tokens, real private reference images, scraped gallery originals, logs with account information or machine-specific paths. Use synthetic test fixtures. Keep generated artifacts out of source commits.
